What is OptimusCMS?

OptimusCMS is a modern content management system built around Open Code: you get 100% ownership of your codebase, at all times. It brings a simpler content experience, performance-focused architecture, and managed hosting together.

What does Open Code let me do?

You own 100% of your codebase, at all times, forever. You can modify it however you want, take it to another host, reuse it, customize it and resell it. Ownership does not expire when you leave.

Is there any restriction on how I use the code?

There is one restriction: you cannot use OptimusCMS or its derivatives to build or operate a service that directly competes with OptimusCMS. This is the boundary of our Open Code philosophy; your ownership, modification, portability and resale rights remain yours.

Is managed hosting included?

Yes. Your OptimusCMS subscription includes managed hosting, ongoing CMS updates and security maintenance. We keep your managed CMS up to date and take care of security updates, so you can focus on using it—not maintaining it.

What am I paying for if I own the code?

Your subscription covers managed hosting, ongoing CMS updates and security maintenance. We look after your managed CMS while you use it. That does not limit your ownership: you can modify your code, take it elsewhere, reuse it, and resell it. The only restriction is using OptimusCMS or its derivatives to build or operate a service that directly competes with OptimusCMS.

How does the free period and pricing work?

Your first 7 days are completely free. After that, the plan is $25.50 per month through month 6. After 6 months, it becomes $49 per month. Managed hosting is included at every stage.

What does free WordPress migration include?

WordPress data migration is free. Moving or recreating your website’s design is a separate scope and is quoted separately. The details of your existing website can be reviewed when planning the move.

Can I bring my existing design?

Design migration is scoped and quoted for your project. It is not included in free WordPress data migration or in the monthly subscription price.

How does OptimusCMS support SEO and growth?

OptimusCMS provides a simpler, performance-focused foundation for your website and content. SEO also depends on useful content, site structure, thoughtful migration planning, and ongoing marketing. A CMS alone cannot guarantee rankings or traffic.
