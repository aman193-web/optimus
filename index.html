What is Optimus?

Optimus is a modern CMS with an open-source core and a focus on simplicity, performance, and security-conscious architecture. Its managed subscription includes hosting, giving you an alternative to a plugin-heavy website setup.

Is managed hosting included?

Yes. Managed hosting is included in the Optimus subscription. You don’t need to arrange a separate hosting service for this plan.

What am I paying for if the CMS is open source?

The CMS core is open source. The subscription is the managed offering, which includes hosting. It brings the CMS and its hosting foundation together in one plan.

What happens after the first 6 months?

You pay $24.50 per month for the first six months—a 50% discount on the standard subscription. From month seven, the subscription is $49 per month.

What does free WordPress migration include?

WordPress data migration is free. Moving or recreating your website’s design is a separate scope and is quoted separately. The details of your existing website can be reviewed when planning the move.

Can I bring my existing design?

Design migration is scoped and quoted for your project. It is not included in free WordPress data migration or in the monthly subscription price.

How does Optimus support SEO and growth?

Optimus provides a simpler, performance-focused foundation for your website and content. SEO also depends on useful content, site structure, thoughtful migration planning, and ongoing marketing. A CMS alone cannot guarantee rankings or traffic.
