## Content — final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: a near-square, high-resolution illustration for the Content tab in the "Meet your Modern Website System" section of the OptimusCMS website. This is the master image for a coordinated set of three.
Create one polished, minimal architectural 3D product illustration of a modern website content canvas. A single upright, gently rounded, frosted mint glass browser panel stands on a very low forest-green rectangular base. Its page layout contains an image tile, three short horizontal text-like strokes, and two smaller content tiles. One small content tile floats just in front of the panel, implying editing; everything is one coherent product sculpture.
Framing: square 1536 by 1536 composition. Three-quarter view from slightly above, restrained perspective. Entire sculpture occupies about 68% of image width and 64% of height. Center horizontally, with the visual center slightly above the image center. Keep every edge fully inside frame, 16% clear margin at left and right, 14% above, 20% below for an HTML caption overlay. No huge close-up, no cropped objects.
Background: uniform very pale warm sage #f0f5ed seamless studio with a subtle floor shadow, almost white, no horizon line. Clean luminous daylight from upper left. Crisp readable edges and smooth premium materials, not noisy, not cloudy. Rich matte forest-green #064b38 for base and selected small elements, translucent pale mint glass, one tiny chartreuse #dcee7b accent. Most of the scene is light and airy.
The result should feel precise and editorial, a designed product illustration, not a collection of icons or a toy. No text, letters, numerals, logo, watermark, humans, devices, keys, archives, gears, shield, padlock, particles, metallic gold, decorative props, excessive glow or saturated green background. Single image, not a collage.
