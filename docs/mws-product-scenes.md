# Modern Website System — product scenes

The section now uses three responsive HTML/CSS concept illustrations. All share a square sage canvas, fine dot grid, tilted workspace card, chartreuse note, and caption. They are concept illustrations, not claims about a completed portal.

- Content: editorial workspace with a generated cover photo.
- Foundation: a vector connection map for content, OptimusCMS, the website and managed hosting.

Scenes use the existing accessible tabs and finite opacity/transform transitions. Reduced motion and Pause Motion disable the transitions. Container-relative typography keeps the composition proportional. Earlier generated sculpture assets are retained but unused.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: ads-marketing.
Asset type: editorial cover image inside a concept CMS content editor on OptimusCMS.
Create a sophisticated abstract editorial still life, photographed as real folded paper and natural light. A single broad ribbon of deep forest green matte paper curves into a gentle arch over warm ivory paper, with a small pale lime circular paper shape partially tucked behind the arch. Soft diagonal window shadows cross the ivory surface. Restrained Swiss editorial art direction, sculptural but beautifully simple, tactile paper edges, fine grain, rich near-black green and warm cream. Close composition, landscape 3:2, high resolution. Keep the main arch in the central area for a wide horizontal crop. No glass, gloss, screens, browser windows, devices, text, letters, numerals, logos, watermarks, metallic objects, glitter, people or extra props. The result should look like a thoughtful magazine cover photograph, not a generic technology illustration.
